Services

Support organized around what you need to do in daily life.

Areas We Support

Find the category closest to your concern

You do not need to know the exact clinical term before reaching out. These groups are designed to make the service list easier to scan.

Children & Learning

Support for speech clarity, language development, learning, and communication at home or school.

  • Articulation
  • Phonological disorders
  • Apraxia
  • Expressive and receptive language
  • Language development
  • Grammar
  • Literacy and phonics
  • Pragmatics and social communication
  • AAC

Adults & Rehabilitation

Support for communication or swallowing changes related to illness, injury, or neurological conditions.

  • Adult swallowing
  • Traumatic brain injury (TBI)
  • Neurological disorders
  • Motor speech disorders
  • Word finding
  • Voice
  • AAC

Voice, Fluency & Communication

Focused therapy for speaking comfort, fluency, vocal health, and functional communication.

  • Stuttering
  • Vocal hygiene
  • Vocal resonance
  • Voice
  • Social communication
  • Pragmatics

For Schools

School contract services

Brave Speech and Language also works with school districts. Heather's experience in both medical and school settings supports a practical understanding of different environments and student needs.

On-site and remote options

School contract support is an active secondary focus of the practice. On-site and remote arrangements can be discussed with districts across Montana based on current availability and district needs.
