Our Approach

Communication affects how people learn, connect, work, recover, and participate in daily life. Our goal is to help each person become more confident, more understood, and supported in the places that matter most: home, school, work, and in the community.

Who We Help

Support for communication needs across different stages of life

Brave Speech and Language supports children, adults, older patients, and caregivers with communication needs that affect daily life.

01

Families with Children

Support for speech sounds, language development, literacy, social communication, AAC, or being understood more clearly at home or school.

02

Adults

Therapy for communication, voice, fluency, swallowing, or language changes after illness, injury, or ongoing difficulty.

03

Older Patients & Caregivers

Practical support for communication, swallowing, voice, memory-related language concerns, stroke recovery, or Parkinson's-related needs and caregiver concerns.

Services

Support for all ages

We provide evaluation and therapy for a wide range of speech, language, voice, swallowing, and communication needs.

Children & Families

Support for articulation, phonological delay, language development, literacy, phonics, social communication, AAC, and childhood apraxia of speech.

Adults & Rehabilitation

Therapy for stroke rehabilitation, traumatic brain injury, word finding, voice, swallowing, and communication changes after illness or injury.

Voice, Fluency & Communication

Support for stuttering, vocal hygiene, vocal resonance, Parkinson's-related communication needs, and SPEAK OUT! therapy

Getting Started

What to expect when you reach out

1. Start with a conversation

We begin by learning what you or your family member is experiencing, what concerns brought you here, and what kind of support may be needed.

2. Complete an evaluation

When therapy is appropriate, we use careful evaluation to understand communication, speech, language, voice, swallowing, or rehabilitation needs.

3. Build a practical plan

Treatment is individualized around real-life goals, whether that means being better understood, communication after injury, supporting school success, or improving daily quality of life.
