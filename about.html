About Brave Speech and Language

Individualized care for communication needs.

Brave Speech and Language is based in Great Falls and supports children, adults, older patients, families, and caregivers through practical, individualized speech-language therapy.

What matters in therapy

Progress should be useful outside the appointment.

Communication and swallowing needs can affect confidence, learning, independence, relationships, and daily routines. Treatment is built around goals that matter in real life.

Our Approach

Experienced care that stays connected to everyday life

Our clinical background includes medical, rehabilitation, and school settings, giving the practice experience with a wide range of communication and swallowing needs.

Therapy is shaped around the person in front of us and the situations that matter outside the therapy room—at home, at school, at work, and in the community.

In-person care is available in Great Falls, with virtual services available across Montana and California when appropriate.
